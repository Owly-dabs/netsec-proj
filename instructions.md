The goal of the project is to Design, implement, and demonstrate/evaluate attack/anomaly detection system using AI/machine-learning technologies. To do so, you should try to reproduce detection mechanisms published in academic papers. A stretch goal would be introducing original modifications that improve the effectiveness of the detection or make it more customized to the use case. 

You may choose one dataset from the following datasets. You can refer to [this link](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/itrust/itrust-labs/datasets/dataset-characteristics/swat/#tabs) for more information on each of the datasets. 
- [Secure Water Treatment (SWaT)](https://sutdapac-my.sharepoint.com/:f:/g/personal/itrust_sutd_edu_sg/EijnugJpDP1Km9yR1enq4igB-02WFY46LCcLA4tqjmMB3g?e=oSKGYh)​
- [Water Distribution (WaDi)​](https://sutdapac-my.sharepoint.com/:f:/g/personal/itrust_sutd_edu_sg/EiYnIxyvOdREgo0NjPUI7dcBXDxoGosa37Wkam7xRd4rTQ)

Do research on each of the datasets. Determine what kind of detection mechanisms, whether from literature or original ideation, could be applicable to each of them. 
Then, determine a few candidates for this project (dataset, along with proposed detection mechanism and proposed implementation plan) and present the candidates in their own html pages.
You can use subagents to do parallel work on the research for each project.
